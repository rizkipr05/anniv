To my dearest partner,

If you're reading this, then I guess the little website I made for our 100-day anniversary is working properly. Hehehe.

Honestly, when we first formed a bond, I never expected we'd make it this far. After what happened with my previous partnerships, I had already prepared myself for the possibility that you might suddenly stop playing the game or even sever our bond because you didn't like my personality.

Yet somehow... you're still here with me.

I still remember how awkward we were at the beginning. You were always the one who greeted me first and kept trying to find something for us to talk about. And hey, you can't really blame me for being so quiet, okay?! Before we became partners, we're practically strangers! We had only known each other for a day, before you suddenly offered to become my partner. Of course I was still shy and trying to behave myself during the early days of our partnership QAQ

I hope we can stay together for a much, much longer time. Because, just like I told you before, even though our partnership only exist in-game, I'll always treat you with the sincerity and care that a partner deserves.

And lastly, happy birthday, dear. May every day bring you more happiness, love, and wonderful memories 🩷

I'm really happy that you were born into this world. And out of all the billions of people in this world, I'm glad that fate brought the two of us together.

From your beloved,
